# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the Sangeet community a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful, considerate, and constructive.
- Welcoming newcomers and helping them learn.
- Giving and accepting feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project.
- Respecting different opinions and experiences.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include harassment, insults, personal attacks, discrimination, publishing private information without permission, and deliberately disruptive conduct.

## Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with it. Maintainers may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces and whenever someone is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the project maintainers through GitHub's private vulnerability/security reporting channel where appropriate, or contact the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review reports fairly and confidentially, take action appropriate to the circumstances, and explain decisions where possible. Maintainers are expected to respect the privacy and safety of reporters.
